Vitamin C: What do you really need to know?

The everyday essential that does more than you think.

3 MIN READ
C

We all know vitamin C is important. But beyond its reputation for supporting immunity, what does it actually do for our bodies? And how much do we really need?

Let's break it down.

01

More than just immune support.

Vitamin C plays a role in several essential processes in the body. It helps maintain normal immune function, supports collagen production, and acts as an antioxidant, protecting cells from oxidative stress.

Collagen is especially important for healthy skin, blood vessels, bones, and connective tissue. So while vitamin C is often associated with fighting colds, its benefits go much further.

02

How much do you actually need?

Daily vitamin C needs vary depending on age, lifestyle and individual circumstances.

80 mg EU daily nutrient reference value for vitamin C in adults.

And the good news? Getting enough doesn't have to be complicated.

Foods like kiwis, strawberries, bell peppers, broccoli, and citrus fruits are naturally rich in vitamin C.

03

Can you have too much?

GOOD TO KNOW ✦

More isn't always better.

Vitamin C is water-soluble, meaning the body doesn't store large amounts of it. Taking high doses doesn't automatically translate into better health.

In fact, excessive supplementation can cause digestive discomfort, including nausea and diarrhoea.

04

Food first, supplements when needed.

A balanced diet can provide enough vitamin C for most people. Supplements can be useful when dietary intake is insufficient, but they aren't a replacement for healthy eating.

The goal isn't to take as much as possible. It's to give your body what it actually needs.

05

The bottom line.

Vitamin C is essential, but it doesn't need to be complicated.

Focus on eating a variety of fruits and vegetables, understanding your body's needs, and making informed choices rather than following every new wellness trend.
