VITLS JOURNAL · OUR WORLD

Why we decided to create VITLS

A simple idea. A fresh perspective. A different way to experience vitamins.

3 MIN READ
VITLS VITAMIN DRINK ✦ BERRY RUSH

Taking care of yourself should feel natural, enjoyable and personal. So why does wellness sometimes feel so complicated?

That simple question is at the heart of VITLS. We wanted to explore a different approach to everyday vitamins — one that feels less like an obligation and more like something to look forward to.

01

It started with a simple idea.

Between busy mornings, university, work and everyday responsibilities, finding time for yourself isn't always easy.

Traditional wellness routines can sometimes feel repetitive or overwhelming. We wanted to create a concept that makes vitamins feel more approachable, modern and enjoyable.

THE VITLS IDEA ✦

Wellness should fit into your life.

Not the other way around. Taking care of yourself should feel like a natural part of your day, not another task on your list.

02

What we believe in.

VITLS is built around three simple values. They shape how we think about vitamins, flavor and everyday wellbeing.

✦

Simplicity

Less complexity. More clarity.

♡

Enjoyment

Everyday wellness should be something you enjoy.

◎

Personalisation

Your preferences. Your routine. Your way.

03

More than just vitamins.

We believe that flavor is an important part of the everyday experience. Something that tastes good can make a routine feel more enjoyable.

That's why the VITLS concept brings together vitamins, refreshing flavors and a modern visual identity.

04

Designed around your everyday life.

Everyone has different preferences, routines and goals. What works for one person might not work for another.

Our vision is to give people more freedom to explore vitamins and flavors that suit their lifestyle, while making informed choices about their individual needs.

VITLS is not about replacing a balanced diet or promising overnight results. It's about making everyday wellness feel a little more personal.

05

This is only the beginning.

VITLS is a growing idea with room for new flavors, fresh experiences and creative possibilities.

Through our products, journal and digital experiences, we want to create a space where wellness feels approachable, inspiring and easy to understand.

Because taking care of yourself doesn't have to be complicated. And it definitely doesn't have to be boring.
